SMS Terms

What this is

Paddy's Pastures operates an SMS notification program for the boarders, staff, and management of our boarding facility. Staff and management receive alerts when a boarder sends a message through the Paddy's Pastures web portal. Each alert names the boarder and includes a link to view and respond to the message. Boarders may receive notifications about their horse's care and messages from the barn.

How you sign up

You opt in by checking the SMS notification box and providing your mobile number during account setup in the Paddy's Pastures web portal. You may also reply START, YES, or UNSTOP to a message from us to resume notifications. Consent is not a condition of boarding at Paddy's Pastures or of using the web portal.

How often we text

Message frequency varies based on activity at the barn.

Cost

Msg & data rates may apply. Paddy's Pastures does not charge for these messages; your mobile carrier may.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them. You will get one confirmation and then nothing further. Reply START to resume.

Getting help

Reply HELP to any message, or contact us at (941) 442-5648 or Howdy@paddyspastures.com.

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Supported carriers

Carrier support may change and we cannot guarantee delivery on every carrier.

Your number

You must be the account holder or have the account holder's permission to sign this number up. Tell us if your number changes or transfers to someone else.

Privacy

We use your mobile number only to send the notifications described above. We do not sell or share it for marketing. See our Privacy Policy.
