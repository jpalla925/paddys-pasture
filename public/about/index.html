How boarding works

Every horse receives daily care from our barn staff, including feeding, turnout, and stall cleaning for horses on full board. Each horse's care plan and treatments are tracked in our barn management system, so nothing is missed.

We keep each horse's medical records and care details on file. Boarders use our private online portal to keep their horse's profile up to date and to message the barn directly, and they can choose to receive text notifications.

Text message notifications

Paddy's Pastures sends text messages to people who have opted in:

  • Staff and management receive an alert when a boarder sends a message through the portal. Each alert names the boarder and includes a link to view and respond to the message.
  • Boarders may receive notifications about their horse's care and messages from the barn.

We do not send marketing or promotional texts. Message frequency varies based on activity at the barn. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help. Full details are in our SMS Terms.

How people opt in

Text notifications are optional and are never a condition of boarding with us or using the portal. Portal accounts are created by barn management for people who board or work with us; there is no public sign-up. When someone first signs in to set up their account, they enter their mobile phone number, and the consent checkbox sits directly below that field. It is unchecked by default, and we only text people who check it. The checkbox reads:

Text me barn notifications. By checking this box you agree to receive SMS messages from Paddy's Pastures about boarder messages and horse care at the barn. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. See our SMS Terms and Privacy Policy.

We record the date and time each person opts in or out, along with the version of the SMS Terms they agreed to.
